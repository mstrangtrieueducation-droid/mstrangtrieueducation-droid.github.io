# Listening source review — 22 October 2026

Scope: all 30 tests, 120 sections, 40 weekly pages, 1,200 IELTS questions and 2,400 dictation gaps.

## Changes

- Restored 12 poor scans against their originals. Original assets remain available for comparison; visual-manifest.json records both hashes and fidelity checks. Eight already readable diagrams remain unchanged. All 20 visuals support zoom and were exercised at 390px and 1440px.
- Removed narrator introductions, repeated examples and transition instructions from Tests 1–10 practice transcripts. Five sections had selected gaps outside the trimmed recording: 2/1, 6/1, 7/3, 8/1 and 9/1. Replaced those with phrases from the dialogue. Corrected reviewed OCR errors.
- Corrected source word limits in 1/2, 1/3 and 5/1; restored supplied context in the experience table (6/1), biography (7/4), and question 25 prefix (9/3).
- Migrated unfinished dictation drafts by answer identity; preserved unchanged responses and IELTS answers. Frozen submission payloads are not rewritten.

## Evidence and limits

All source question pages and transcripts were compared with deployed content. Full recording waveform comparisons, accounting for trimmed introductions, identify all 120 recordings as their matching source recordings (minimum aggregate correlation 0.90472). Per-section URLs, durations, offsets and SHA-256 hashes are in source-review-20261022.json. A waveform match is not an independent semantic review of the answer key. Some source pages themselves contain OCR mistakes, so textual differences were reviewed rather than blindly copied.

Public source HTML did not expose official answer keys. Browser tests establish that the configured keys award full marks across all 120 sections; this does not independently certify all 1,200 answers. Test 22/4 has an additional source tail beyond the matching deployed 514-second segment; this tail is not certified by waveform overlap.

## Regression checks

- content-audit.cjs: complete question/gap/section sets, no legacy narrator blocks, unique gap positions, approved visuals, original/restored image hashes, deployed audio hashes matching source review.
- content-runtime.test.cjs: all 40 pages, configured answer grading, preservation and idempotence of old draft migration.
- visual-ui.test.cjs: 20 images at two viewport sizes, image loading, zoom/close and layout.
- Submission contract and auto-submit UI suites also passed locally; CI runs the wider submission regression suite.

The repair scripts are repeatable and idempotent. This review provides specific checks and evidence, not a guarantee that no future content or platform issue can occur.
